Frequently Asked Questions

Before submitting your request

Here are answers to some common questions about custom MQL development.

How much does an MQL project cost?

Pricing depends on project complexity, trading rules, required features, and testing requirements. After reviewing your request, you will receive a clear estimate.

How long does development take?

Small indicators or code fixes may take a few days. More advanced Expert Advisors may require one or several weeks.

Do I receive the source code?

Source code delivery can be included in the project agreement. The exact delivery terms are confirmed before development begins.

Can you guarantee that my strategy will be profitable?

No. FLECTA can accurately implement and test your trading rules, but no Expert Advisor, indicator, or strategy can guarantee profits in financial markets.
