About FLECTA

Built around technical clarity, not unrealistic promises.

FLECTA is focused on developing practical software solutions for traders who want to automate a strategy, build a custom indicator, improve an existing MQL project, or convert an idea into a working trading tool.

We believe successful development begins with clear requirements. Before writing code, the strategy rules, expected behavior, limitations, and testing conditions should be understood as clearly as possible.
